CajaModa Creator Program Agreement

You participate as an independent creator—not as an employee, store owner, partner, agent, franchisee, or legal representative of CajaModa. You control how and when you create content and are responsible for your local laws, disclosures, and taxes.

Commission

Your tier percentage is applied to the non-negative commissionable margin for each attributed item after payment is captured: item sale price collected, excluding delivery, tax, and fees, minus CajaModa's acquisition cost, minus a COP 12,000 marketing reserve per item. An authorization is pending and earns no commission until captured.

Payout schedule

Commissions earned from the 1st–15th are scheduled for payment on or about the last calendar day of that month. Commissions earned from the 16th–month-end are scheduled for payment on or about the 15th of the following month.

Conduct and termination

Either you or CajaModa may end participation at any time. Fraud, theft, scams, chargebacks, manipulation, and unlawful conduct are prohibited. CajaModa may withhold or reverse related commissions, remove participants, and take lawful action to recover losses. CajaModa is a U.S. company and does not offer discretionary refunds, except where applicable law requires one.
