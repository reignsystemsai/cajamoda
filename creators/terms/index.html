Creator Program Agreement

Version: September 13, 2026

1. Program relationship

You join the CajaModa Creator Program as an independent creator. You are not an employee, store owner, partner, joint venturer, franchisee, agent, or legal representative of CajaModa. You cannot bind CajaModa or make promises on its behalf. You control when, where, and how you create content and use your own resources.

2. Eligibility and compliance

You are responsible for complying with the laws, advertising disclosures, tax obligations, platform rules, and regulations that apply in your country. You must clearly disclose your material relationship with CajaModa whenever required.

3. Attribution and commission

Eligible activity must be attributed to your active personal creator link. Your commission is calculated per item only after payment is captured:

Commission = max(0, item sale price collected − CajaModa acquisition cost − COP 12,000 marketing reserve) × your tier percentage.

Delivery charges, taxes, payment fees, unauthorized payments, and uncaptured authorizations do not earn commission. Your tier and percentage appear in your creator dashboard.

4. Payout timing

Commissions earned from the 1st through the 15th are scheduled for payment on or about the last calendar day of that month. Commissions earned from the 16th through month-end are scheduled for payment on or about the 15th of the following month. Accurate Nequi or PayPal information is required. Processing time outside CajaModa's control may vary.

5. Returns, disputes, and unlawful activity

CajaModa is a United States company and does not offer discretionary refunds, except where applicable law requires one. Fraud, theft, scams, chargebacks, attribution manipulation, misleading statements, and unlawful activity are prohibited. CajaModa is not responsible for losses caused by such conduct. Related commissions may be withheld or reversed, participants may be removed, and CajaModa may take lawful action to investigate and recover losses.

6. Content and brand use

You may use CajaModa names and approved materials only to promote the program truthfully. You must not misrepresent products, pricing, delivery, earnings, or your relationship with CajaModa.

7. Termination

You or CajaModa may end the creator relationship at any time, with or without cause, subject to applicable law. After termination, your link and portal access may be disabled. Valid commissions already earned remain subject to this agreement.

8. Electronic acceptance

Checking the acceptance box and selecting “Accept and Continue” is your electronic signature. CajaModa records your name, verified email, agreement version, acceptance text, timestamp, IP address, and browser information as evidence of acceptance.
